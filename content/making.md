## Niceties

Niceties is a home for tools I make to improve everyday work, starting with reading research papers on the web.

### IN DEVELOPMENT | Paper Night

Paper Night is a browser extension for **INSPIRE, arXiv, bioRxiv, PubMed, and PubMed Central**. It gives these sites gentler reading palettes while preserving the original colors in figures.

There are four palettes—Charcoal, High Contrast, Midnight, and Lamplight—with independent link colors and controls for each site. It can follow the device’s appearance, stay dark, or restore the website’s original appearance.

Settings stay in the browser. There are no accounts or analytics. It is currently a local prototype; PDF viewers and embedded documents are outside its scope.
