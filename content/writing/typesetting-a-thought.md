A personal page can have the quiet structure of a paper while leaving room for a less formal thought. This paragraph is here to test the width of a line, the space between sentences, and the feeling of reading a few paragraphs on screen.

## 1. A familiar starting point

For a discrete collection of states with energies $E_i$, the canonical distribution is

$$
p_i = \frac{e^{-\beta E_i}}{Z}, \qquad Z = \sum_j e^{-\beta E_j}.
$$

Here $\beta = 1/(k_\mathrm{B}T)$. The equation sits in the flow of the text, with enough space to read it comfortably. Inline mathematics should feel equally at home in a sentence.

## 2. And a longer calculation

Starting from the definition of the partition function,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial \log Z}{\partial\beta}
&= \frac{1}{Z}\sum_i (-E_i)e^{-\beta E_i} \\
&= -\langle E\rangle.
\end{aligned}
$$

This is also a place for an ordinary argument, a reading response, or an observation that does not require an equation at all. The name of the section should leave those possibilities open.
