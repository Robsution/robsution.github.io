Department of Physics, Washington University in St. Louis

Physics PhD student & Arthur L. Hughes Fellow

Abstract

I’m interested in how mathematical models describe complex systems, especially in theoretical biophysics and condensed matter. My current work ranges from bacterial auxotrophy and spin glasses to machine learning in nuclear and particle physics.

Condensed matter

I’m a TA for Physics 1760, Focused Physics I, at WashU. At Princeton, I was an undergraduate course assistant in mechanics and electromagnetism (2023–2024), and a peer tutor in mechanics and quantum mechanics (2023–2024).

3. Things I make

I’m developing Paper Night, a browser extension for reading physics and biology papers with a gentler background while preserving the colors in figures. It’s part of Niceties, a collection of tools I’m making for everyday use.
